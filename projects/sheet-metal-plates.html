Sheet Metal Mounting Plates

Convergent Dental · Mechanical Engineering Intern · Sketch, SOLIDWORKS, fit to chassis, release drawings, prototype

Sheet-metal plates that mount the air module and the galvos inside a new laser chassis, built to cut manufacturing time and make repairs simpler for field engineers.

The problem

Convergent was moving to a new internal chassis structure for the Helios laser to reduce manufacturing time and repair complexity for field engineers. I designed the sheet-metal plates that mount two modules inside it: 1. the air module and 2. the galvos.

1. Sketch

I sketched layouts for each module to work out where the parts, fasteners and access points should go.

2. SOLIDWORKS

I modeled both plates as sheet-metal parts in SOLIDWORKS.

3. Fit to chassis

I mounted each module on its plate in the chassis assembly and checked that both fit inside the new structure.

4. Release drawings

For each plate I released an assembly drawing, a dimensioned part drawing and a flat pattern.

5. Prototype

Both plates were made as sheet-metal prototypes.
