Sheet Metal Mounting Plates

Convergent Dental · Mechanical Engineering Intern · Concept, CAD, drawings and prototype

Compact mounting plates that tidy the inside of a dental laser and make it faster to service.

The problem

Inside a dental laser, components compete for space and field engineers need to reach them quickly.

Concepts

I sketched layouts for the air module and for the galvo and scan electronics before moving into CAD.

Air-module plate

A bent plate that carries the air filters and regulator, iterated in SOLIDWORKS to fit the existing chassis.

Galvo plate

A second, smaller plate holds the galvo electronics on one bent bracket, so they mount and come out as a single unit.

Result

Both plates were released with drawings and built as sheet-metal prototypes, giving a tidier layout that is simpler to assemble and service.
