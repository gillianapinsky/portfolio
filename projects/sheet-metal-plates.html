02  CONVERGENT DENTAL · INTERNSHIP

Sheet Metal Mounting Plates

Designed and validated compact sheet-metal mounting plates to improve accessibility, manufacturability and serviceability within the dental laser chassis.

Role
Mechanical Engineering Intern
Context
Convergent Dental, Helios laser
Tools
SOLIDWORKSSheet metal designAssembly designDFM

Problem

Convergent Dental was moving the Helios laser to a new internal chassis structure to decrease manufacturing time and reduce repair complexity for field engineers. Inside the chassis, components compete for limited space and must be quick to reach for service.

I designed the sheet-metal plates that mount two modules inside it: 1. the air module and 2. the galvos, taking each from sketch to physical prototype.

+ [ DESIGN PROCESS ]

01Concept Generation

I sketched mounting layouts for both modules before modeling.

02Iterative CAD Design

I modeled both plates as sheet-metal parts in SOLIDWORKS and iterated them for fit and manufacturability.

03Fit to Chassis

I mounted each module on its plate in the SOLIDWORKS chassis assembly to check fit inside the new structure.

04Release Drawings

I released a drawing package for each plate: an assembly drawing with BOM, a dimensioned part drawing and a flat pattern. Specified material: aluminum 5052-H32, 0.1 in (2.54 mm) thick, with a clear chromate finish per MIL-C-5541 Type II, Class 1A (RoHS).

05Final Sheet Metal Prototype

Both plates were fabricated as physical sheet-metal prototypes and physically fit-tested in the chassis.

Results & Impact

  • Designed two sheet-metal mounting plates, for the air module and the galvos, within the redesigned Helios chassis.
  • Released assembly drawings with BOMs, dimensioned part drawings and flat patterns for fabrication.
  • Produced physical sheet-metal prototypes of both plates and fit-tested them in the chassis.
  • Design goal: improve internal layout efficiency and simplify assembly and maintenance for field service.
