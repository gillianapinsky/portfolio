Welded Sheet Metal Organizer

A heavy-duty steel rack with 19 evenly spaced dividers, drawn as a three-sheet package and welded by hand.

Role
Design, drawings, cutting and welding
Context
Fabrication course
Tools
Sheet metalMIG weldingTechnical drawing
36 × 16 × 10 inoverall size
19dividers, 1.9 in apart
20parts

Design

A 1/8 in steel base holds 19 dividers cut from 14 gauge sheet. A 3 in chamfer on each divider opens the front of every slot so items slide in without snagging.

Drawings and welding

I drew the package with a BOM and weld note, then cut the parts and welded each divider with short 2 in welds on each side, which hold it rigid while limiting warping.
