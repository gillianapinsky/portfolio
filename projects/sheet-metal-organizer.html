Welded Sheet Metal Organizer

A heavy-duty steel rack with 19 evenly spaced dividers, drawn as a three-sheet package and welded by hand.

Role

Design

A single steel base plate holds 19 vertical dividers. I documented it as a fabrication drawing package, then cut the parts and welded them together.

ItemPartQtySize
1Vertical plate (divider)1916 × 10 in, 14 ga (0.07 in), 3 in × 45° chamfer
2Main plate (base)136 × 16 in, 1/8 in thick
  • Thick base, thin dividers: the 1/8 in base gives weight and stiffness so the rack won't tip or flex; 14 gauge dividers keep it no heavier than needed.
  • Chamfered front corner: a 3 in, 45° cut on each divider opens the front of every slot so items slide in without snagging.
  • Short welds, not full seams: a 2 in weld on each side of each divider holds it rigid while limiting heat that warps thin plate.
